The question

When an image changes in a way that should not change its meaning, a reliable model should keep the same answer. A useful benchmark measures where that expectation breaks down and what kinds of visual evidence the model was using.

The article layout leaves room for explanation, citations, figures, and the occasional aside. A real post would link its claims to papers and show the actual methods and results.
